Mr Moo Uploader

Mr Moo Uploader is a small personal tool used by the Mr Moos Clips YouTube channel. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and uploads the owner's short cat videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, scheduled for publication at set times each day.

It is not a public service: nobody else can sign in to it or use it, and it does not access any other person's account or data.
