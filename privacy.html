Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Mr Moo Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Mr Moos Clips YouTube channel to upload their own videos to their own channel.

What data the app uses

When the channel owner signs in, the app receives permission from Google to upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload) and to read the name of the signed-in channel (youtube.readonly). It uses these permissions only to upload the owner's videos and to confirm which channel they go to.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token and a log of uploaded videos (video IDs, titles and publish times) are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use data for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data.
